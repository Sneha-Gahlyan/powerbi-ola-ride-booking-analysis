# 🚗 Ola Ride Booking Analysis | Power BI Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing Ola ride booking data using **Microsoft Power BI** to understand ride performance, customer behavior, revenue generation, vehicle preferences, cancellations, and customer and driver ratings.

The interactive dashboard transforms raw ride booking data into meaningful business insights through KPIs, charts, tables, and date filters. It helps identify booking trends, evaluate vehicle performance, analyze payment preferences, and understand cancellation patterns.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze ride booking volume and booking status trends.
- Evaluate vehicle types based on ride distance.
- Understand revenue distribution across payment methods.
- Identify customers contributing the highest booking value.
- Investigate customer and driver cancellation reasons.
- Compare customer and driver ratings to assess service quality.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI** – Dashboard development and data visualization
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation, where applicable
- **DAX** – Measures and KPI calculations, where applicable
- **Data Analysis** – Trend analysis, aggregation, ranking, and performance evaluation

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Overall Analysis
Provides a high-level overview of ride booking performance, including total bookings, booking status breakdown, and ride volume trends over time.

### 2. Vehicle Type Analysis
Compares vehicle categories using total booking value, successful booking value, average ride distance, and total ride distance.

### 3. Revenue Analysis
Examines revenue by payment method and identifies the top five customers based on total booking value.

### 4. Cancellation Analysis
Analyzes customer and driver cancellation reasons, successful bookings, cancelled bookings, and the overall cancellation rate.

### 5. Ratings Analysis
Compares customer and driver ratings across different vehicle types to evaluate service quality and consistency.

## 🔍 Business Questions Answered

This project addresses the following business questions:

1. **Ride Volume Over Time:** How does the number of bookings change over time?
2. **Booking Status Breakdown:** What proportion of bookings are successful, cancelled, or affected by driver unavailability?
3. **Top 5 Vehicle Types by Ride Distance:** Which vehicle types contribute the highest total ride distance?
4. **Average Customer Ratings by Vehicle Type:** How do customer ratings vary across vehicle categories?
5. **Cancelled Ride Reasons:** What are the most common reasons for customer and driver cancellations?
6. **Revenue by Payment Method:** Which payment methods contribute the most booking value?
7. **Top 5 Customers by Total Booking Value:** Which customers generate the highest booking value?
8. **Ride Distance Distribution Per Day:** How does total ride distance vary daily?
9. **Driver Ratings Distribution:** How are driver ratings distributed across rides?
10. **Customer vs. Driver Ratings:** How do customer and driver ratings compare across vehicle types?

## 🚀 How to Explore the Project

1. Clone or download this repository.
2. Open the `.pbix` file using Microsoft Power BI Desktop.
3. If prompted, configure the data source path and refresh the data.
4. Explore the five dashboard pages using the navigation buttons.
5. Use the date slicers and visual interactions to investigate booking trends, vehicle performance, revenue, cancellations, and ratings.

## 💡 Business Value

This analysis demonstrates how business intelligence tools can be used to monitor ride booking performance, identify revenue patterns, understand cancellation behavior, compare vehicle categories, and evaluate service quality.

The findings can help stakeholders identify areas for operational improvement and make data-informed decisions about customer experience and ride services.
